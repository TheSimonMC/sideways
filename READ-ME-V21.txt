SIDEWAYS / CITY SESSIONS V21

PLAY
Open https://thesimonmc.github.io/sideways/ or extract the ZIP and open SIDEWAYS.html.
The separate SIDEWAYS-v21.html contains identical game bytes. Single-player works
offline. Multiplayer needs signaling/peer connectivity; every driver needs V21.
Progress stays in the browser and origin where you played.

PHASE 0 / PHASE 1A + 1B + 1C + 1D / PHASE 2A + 2B BRAKES / BEARINGS / STEERING
The full requested roadmap is in docs/goal.txt. See docs/goal-progress.md for all
156 phase requirements and their explicit status. Phase 0 is complete; Phase 1 and Phase 2
are partial. There is no free handling-mode selector. Steering, alignment and
anti-roll bars are garage parts that affect the existing four-wheel solver.
Traction control only reduces throttle; ABS remains fitted. Input profiles and
saves are retained, and old handling values are ignored.

HILLS AND ROAD CONTACT
Existing hills now affect rollback, acceleration and braking through contact
forces. Reset aligns the car to the road without a false suspension impact.
Individual bumps feed the existing wheels and suspension sounds. Airborne
wheels receive no horizontal force from terrain below. Vehicle condition,
parts and maps remain intact. Phase 1 is still partial: weather and broader calibration remain unfinished.
Next is Phase 2C: belts, alternator/battery, radiator fan and other engine auxiliaries.
See docs/phase-2b-chassis-report.md for evidence and technical limits.

DRIVETRAIN CONVERSIONS
Vector with I4/V6 supports paid FWD. Hachi/Kitsune/Vector without V10/V12
support AWD with nominal open 40:60 torque split, front and center LSD options.
These parts alter the actual torque paths, mass, heat, wear and repair state.
Incompatible previews explain the fallback to factory RWD. Ownership and
damage persist; changing the fitted drive kit does not repair stored components.
All original factory cars remain RWD. Multiplayer protocol is 15.

AERO AND AXLE LINKS
GT wings support 2/6/14-degree mounts. Spoilers, front splitters and rear diffusers
alter real downforce, drag, axle loading and fitted mass. The garage compares
healthy-part forces at 100 km/h. Damage reduces aero; a detached spoiler loses
its effect and stays missing until repaired. Changing mounts does not repair it.
Braced axle links reduce traction-induced wheel hop for 3,200 points and 3 kg.
This is a bounded modal approximation, not a full suspension linkage model.
Installed masses count; detached debris mass is not subtracted from the chassis.

TYRE PRESSURE AND DAMAGE
Each tyre stores its own pressure charge, casing condition, puncture and oriented
flat spot. Heat and sustained slip can cause a blowout with real grip, radius,
rolling-resistance and suspension changes. The rim stays attached. Rubber changes
shape; a fresh burst adds a short procedural sound and bounded rubber particles.
Open Vehicle Condition to see pressure, casing and flat-spot readouts. A mechanic
can replace an individual tyre without repairing suspension damage. Buying a new
compound preserves casing damage; replacement restores 2.3 bar cold. There is no
separate inflation control yet. Road recovery and saving preserve tyre damage.
This is a lumped model with one dominant flat spot, not a real-car calibration.
Remote multiplayer vehicles do not receive this detailed tyre state yet.

MULTIPLAYER RATE_LIMIT FIX
Both players should reload V21 before creating or joining a lobby. The tested fix
coalesces position updates that arrive together after a loading/render stall.
Control-message flood protection remains active. Protocol stays compatible at 15.
Real WebRTC and two local browser processes were tested; other networks and TURN
fallback may still need separate investigation.

BRAKE LINES AND FLUID
Wheel impacts can damage a hose; a detached wheel tears it. The FL/RR and FR/RL
circuits have separate fluid reservoirs. A leak reduces real foot-brake force;
the mechanical handbrake remains independent. Vehicle Condition shows four hoses
and two fluid levels. Replace the leaking hose, then refill its circuit. Refill
alone does not seal a leak. Upgrades and tyre replacements preserve hose damage.
Hot discs glow from their calculated temperature, separately at each wheel.
V21 adds master-cylinder seals, booster assistance and fail-passive ABS faults.
Disc heat can boil fluid; degraded fluid boils earlier. An emptied circuit admits
air. Refill does not remove air: refill and bleed it. Fluid change replaces aged
fluid. Pedal travel is exposed as a normalized diagnostic value. These are lumped
approximations with shared pad/disc condition, not real-car calibration.

WHEEL BEARINGS AND STEERING
Damaged wheel bearings add friction, heat, wheel play and rumble. A seized bearing
can lock its wheel. Bent tie rods change alignment; broken rods disconnect a wheel
from the rack. A seized rack holds its angle. Existing suspension damage stays
separate. All four car models show steering links and actual wheel alignment.
At a mechanic, replace only the damaged part in Vehicle Condition. Tyre replacement
does not heal a bearing or tie rod; changing a steering kit does not repair damage.
Recovery and saves preserve these faults; Restart restores the whole car.

RESTART AND PAUSE
Starting a new run or choosing Pause > Restart restores the car, including wheels,
mechanical condition, fuel and NOS. Road recovery (R) keeps existing damage.
Escape opens Pause while driving. Escape again resumes; Main Menu is an explicit
button in Pause. Nested screens still return to their previous screen.

NEON ARCHIPELAGO
Four landmasses joined by five bridges. 103.1 square kilometres including water,
approximately 52.5 km² of land and 266 km of roads including alleys. Seventeen
service stops. Six additional districts connect to downtown, expressway and hills.
Palm Beach, Westhaven, Sunset Keys, suburbs and industry use separate architectural
styles, with 2,244 additional buildings assembled from façade and roof modules.
Roads climb to 210 m. The separate Dock 09 track still hosts racing and drift modes.
This is an original low-poly world, not a GTA replica or equivalent simulation.

VISIBLE TUNING
Choose hood, spoiler, side skirts, rims and front/rear bumpers in the garage.
Previewing costs nothing. Buy & Install shows the total and keeps the parts owned.
All four cars support the six visual categories. Old saves default to factory parts.
Hoods, skirts and rims remain visual. Spoilers and bumpers also affect aero and
mass. Direct engine, gearbox and audio-source parameters remain unchanged.
Water recovery returns you to a road and retains damage, like R.

BUILD AND VERIFY
With Node.js 24: npm ci; node build.mjs.
The build produces dist/SIDEWAYS.html. Run qa/standalone-v21-check.mjs and
all seven *browser-v21.cjs checks plus the native regression suites before
qa/package-v21.mjs. See README.md for the current commands.
Browser scripts need Playwright. CODEX_PRIMARY_RUNTIME_NODE_MODULES points to its
node_modules directory; PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE selects a compatible browser.
qa/release-v21.json lists current test reports. The package script checks the exact
HTML hash against browser evidence. Copy dist/SIDEWAYS.html to SIDEWAYS.html before
committing for GitHub Pages; the workflow checks the same hash before deploying.

LIMITS
QA uses software-rendered Chromium and automated keyboard input. Physical gamepads,
wheels, speaker listening and live cross-network multiplayer were not checked.
Road geometry tests cover routing, grades, clearances and sampled wheel positions;
they do not mean every metre was manually driven. The SDK remains MotorLab 1.6.3
with the existing SIDEWAYS spatial extension. Its prior replay-fixture hash mismatch
is documented in qa/sdk-v12-baseline.json. Damage is not full soft-body physics.
