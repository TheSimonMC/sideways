SIDEWAYS V24 – RAIN AND WET ROADS

Open SIDEWAYS.html to play offline. Multiplayer requires internet.
Online: https://thesimonmc.github.io/sideways/

Settings > Weather selects changing weather, clear skies or rain.
Rain and drying take time. Restart repairs the car but keeps road moisture.
Wet grip and puddles depend on surface, speed, tyre tread, pressure and compound.
All multiplayer clients need V24. Host weather is shared, including late joins.
The existing RATE_LIMIT fix remains in place.

Report: docs/phase-1e-weather-report.md
Full goal/checklist: docs/goal.txt and docs/goal-progress.md
Evidence: qa/release-v24.json
Existing licenses: dist/audio/CREDITS.md and source credits. New rain is procedural.

Build: npm ci, then node build.mjs (Node.js 24).
Native checks: node qa/regressions-v24.mjs and the *-v24-check.mjs files.
Browser checks need Playwright and Chromium. Set CODEX_PRIMARY_RUNTIME_NODE_MODULES
to node_modules containing playwright and PLAYWRIGHT_CHROMIUM_EXECUTABLE to Chromium.
Run the *-browser-v24.cjs files. Package: node qa/package-v24.mjs <output-directory>.
