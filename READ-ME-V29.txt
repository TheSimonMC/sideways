SIDEWAYS V29 – COCKPITS AND INTERIORS

Open SIDEWAYS.html for offline single-player. Multiplayer requires internet.
Online: https://thesimonmc.github.io/sideways/

All four original cars have fitted interiors, live instruments, a fourth
camera, navigation/music controls, rear-view mirrors and an animated driver.
C cycles cameras. Settings > Drive adjusts seat, FOV, head motion and wheel,
hand and mirror visibility. U toggles music; [ and ] adjust volume. You can
also click the actual radio display or assign controller buttons.
Controls > Calibration offers hardware wheel rotation after axis calibration.
Use the same total rotation as your device driver. Factory Range preserves
the existing full fitted-rack range. No physical wheel/FFB test is claimed.
